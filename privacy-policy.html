Privacy Policy

App: Personal Vault
Effective date: November 3, 2026
Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Personal Vault (the “App”) handles information when you use it. The App is designed to store vault information on your device and does not use an app-operated backend or account service.

1. Information you enter

You may choose to enter or save information such as passwords, notes, documents, and family or emergency information. This content is stored locally in the App's data storage on your device. It is not transmitted to a server operated by the App developer as part of the App's normal vault functionality.

2. Information collection and sharing

The App developer does not operate a backend service for the App and does not receive the vault content you enter through the App's normal functionality. The developer does not sell your vault content.

3. Storage, security, and your responsibility

Your vault content is stored in the App's private storage on your device. Device security features, such as your screen lock, can help protect access to the device.

Important security disclosure: The current app implementation may store vault records and files without application-level encryption. Do not treat this App as providing encrypted-at-rest protection unless and until that feature has been implemented and verified in the released version. Keep your device and operating system up to date, use a strong device screen lock, and do not use the App as the only copy of critical information.

Anyone who gains access to an unlocked device or otherwise obtains access to the App's local data may be able to access information stored by the App. You are responsible for securing your device and any copies or exports you create.

4. Backups and device transfers

The App is configured to exclude its vault database and designated vault files from Android cloud backup and device-transfer mechanisms. This is intended to reduce the chance of vault content being copied through those mechanisms. Android, device manufacturers, or other apps may have their own backup or transfer behavior outside the developer's control. Do not assume that your vault data is backed up or recoverable.

5. Retention and deletion

Your information remains in the App's local storage until you delete it, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App, subject to Android and device-specific behavior. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data may permanently remove locally stored vault content. The developer cannot restore locally stored information because the developer does not operate a vault-content server.

6. Permissions and files

If you choose to import, scan, view, or share documents, Android may ask for relevant permissions or show a system file/photo picker. The information you select is used for the feature you requested. Review Android's permission prompts and the destination app before sharing a file.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not specifically directed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information through an app-operated backend.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be posted at this page with an updated “Last updated” date. Check this page periodically.
